# Privacy Check

Use this checklist before publishing any dispatch.

## Personal identifiers

- [ ] No private names.
- [ ] No private phone numbers.
- [ ] No private email addresses.
- [ ] No private addresses or locations.
- [ ] No account IDs or handles unless intentionally public and necessary.

## Work confidentiality

- [ ] No internal company information.
- [ ] No non-public product strategy.
- [ ] No internal roadmap.
- [ ] No private metrics.
- [ ] No customer, partner, or vendor-sensitive data.
- [ ] No internal screenshots, quotes, or document excerpts.

## Infrastructure and security

- [ ] No IP addresses.
- [ ] No tokens, keys, passwords, cookies, or secrets.
- [ ] No internal URLs, private hostnames, VPN details, or topology.
- [ ] No operational procedures that expose sensitive infrastructure.

## Personal finance and home context

- [ ] No exact personal balances, expenses, or account details.
- [ ] No private purchase inventory as public disclosure.
- [ ] No exact home infrastructure, address, or household-sensitive detail.

## Images and metadata

- [ ] No screenshots with private UI or accounts.
- [ ] No EXIF metadata from personal photos.
- [ ] No embedded private file IDs where avoidable.
- [ ] Images are either generated, properly licensed, or used only as source-linked analytical references.

## Editorial quality

- [ ] Facts, reviews, opinions, signals, rumors, forecasts, and marketing claims are clearly separated.
- [ ] Major claims have sources.
- [ ] Community sentiment is not presented as fact.
- [ ] Buying recommendations include conditions and criteria.
- [ ] The piece is useful even without knowing the private context.

## Decision

- [ ] Draft only.
- [ ] Safe for private archive.
- [ ] Safe for public mirror.
- [ ] Needs redaction.
