# Task: Curated Daily Digest

Create a curated analytical digest from:

- `validation/reviewed-radar-latest.md`
- `validation/auto-dispatches/`

Do not publish automatically. Do not move output into `dispatches/` unless the
task explicitly says publication and the user has approved it.

## Required Structure

1. Главное за период.
2. События с наибольшим эффектом.
3. Регуляторика и правовой контур.
4. Инфраструктура и участники рынка.
5. Продуктовые и организационные импликации.
6. Радар слабых сигналов.
7. Что проверять дальше.
8. Источники и уровень надёжности.

## Evidence Discipline

Separate these categories clearly:

- fact;
- trend;
- assessment;
- hypothesis;
- unconfirmed signal.

Do not present hypotheses, weak signals or unconfirmed reports as facts.

## Prohibited Content

- No investment advice.
- No legal, tax or compliance advice.
- No internal, company-private or personal details.
- No private prompts, private URLs, account data or credentials.
- No automatic publication.

## Handoff

Return changed files, checks run, risks and ready/not ready status.
