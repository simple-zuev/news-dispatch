# Anti-Advertising Policy

News Dispatch is an editorial product, not an advertising surface.

## Forbidden

Do not publish:

- paid promotion;
- hidden sponsorship;
- affiliate-driven recommendations;
- vendor placement;
- unsupported praise;
- promotional copy;
- urgency pressure;
- unverified superlatives.

## Risky language

Avoid phrases like:

- лучший выбор;
- обязательно брать;
- топ за свои деньги;
- идеальный вариант;
- must-have;
- безальтернативно;
- точно стоит купить;
- всем подходит.

## Allowed editorial assessment

Allowed only when evidence-based and conditional:

- подходит, если;
- не подходит, если;
- стоит наблюдать;
- требует проверки;
- главный риск;
- слабое место;
- сильная сторона;
- сценарий, где это имеет смысл.

## Product and gear coverage

Reviews and buying analysis must separate:

- facts;
- specs;
- long-term ownership signals;
- public user complaints;
- reviewer opinion;
- commercial claims;
- editorial conclusion.

## Disclosure rule

If any commercial relationship, sponsorship or material incentive exists, the material must not be published in News Dispatch unless it is explicitly disclosed and editorially justified.

Default assumption: no advertising, no sponsorship, no affiliate links.
