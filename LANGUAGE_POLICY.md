# Language Policy

News Dispatch is a Russian-first publication.

## Public-facing language

All public-facing editorial copy should be in Russian by default:

- homepage text;
- navigation labels;
- stream descriptions;
- dispatch summaries;
- editorial notes;
- article body text;
- cards;
- archive pages.

## Allowed English

English is allowed when it improves precision or is part of the technical surface:

- project name: `News Dispatch`;
- repository paths;
- YAML front matter keys;
- workflow names and GitHub Actions terms;
- URLs;
- source titles and organization/product names;
- established terms such as EDC, UX, HCI, AI, RSS, OpenGraph, sitemap, front matter;
- quoted source material when needed.

## Avoid mixed-language noise

Do not mix Russian and English when a clean Russian label exists.

Prefer:

- `Последние выпуски` over `Latest dispatches`;
- `Архив выпусков` over `Dispatch archive`;
- `Потоки` over `Streams`;
- `Редакционная проверка` over `Editorial review`;
- `Строгая проверка` over `Strict review`.

## Tone

Russian copy should sound like a serious editorial product, not a literal translation of a software interface.

Avoid:

- machine-translated phrasing;
- overloaded bureaucratic wording;
- assistant-like explanations;
- direct references to private context or intended beneficiaries.

## Current tagline

Use:

> Редакционный журнал о технологиях, рынках, продуктах, инфраструктуре, вещах, городе, культуре и науке.

Do not use:

> Аналитический хаб для обезличенных выпусков о технологиях, рынках, инфраструктуре, вещах, городе, культуре и научном горизонте.

Reason: the older phrase sounds mechanical, overexplains privacy, and reads like a system requirement rather than a publication. The new phrase is shorter, more editorial, and clearer for external readers.
