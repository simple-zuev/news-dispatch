# Work Dispatch

Work Dispatch is a separate stream for work-related analytical briefings.

It is not a place for private company notes, internal product documents, private strategy, internal metrics, customer data, or sensitive operational context.

## Scope

Allowed:

- public market signals;
- public product launches;
- public competitor moves;
- public regulatory and policy changes;
- AI, productivity, UX, product management, org design, and operating model trends;
- anonymized implications for product, team, organization, and leadership;
- general strategic questions and decision criteria.

## Anti-scope

Do not include:

- confidential company information;
- non-public product plans;
- private roadmaps;
- internal metrics;
- private customer or partner information;
- names of colleagues, clients, partners, or vendors unless explicitly public and necessary;
- private legal, compliance, security, financial, or commercial details;
- screenshots or quotes from internal documents;
- private project names if they are not already public.

## Safe framing

Unsafe:

> Our product should do X because internal roadmap Y says Z.

Safe:

> Public market signals suggest a broader product pattern: users increasingly expect X. A product team should evaluate whether this changes prioritization criteria.

Unsafe:

> Competitor A affects our internal launch plan.

Safe:

> Public competitor moves may change user expectations, pricing anchors, distribution assumptions, or roadmap sequencing for teams in the category.

## Output style

Work Dispatch should read like a senior product and market intelligence memo:

- signal;
- source confidence;
- market mechanism;
- product implication;
- organizational implication;
- risks;
- questions for discussion;
- what would change the view.
